# FLUX.1 Kontext Image Editing Models are now available at fal

- [![Team fal](https://storage.ghost.io/c/0e/15/0e15ee8a-bd95-4b71-9258-950f77f4d196/content/images/size/w100/2024/09/Cloock-Frame.png)](https://blog.fal.ai/author/features-and-labels/)

May 29, 2025• 2 min read

![FLUX.1 Kontext Image Editing Models are now available at fal](https://storage.ghost.io/c/0e/15/0e15ee8a-bd95-4b71-9258-950f77f4d196/content/images/size/w2000/2025/05/new-model-post---1x1---17.png)

We are thrilled to announce a new frontier image editing is now available at fal. [FLUX.1 Kontext](http://fal.ai/flux-kontext?ref=blog.fal.ai) just joined our platform, and this isn't your average image model. This multimodal flow model understands both text AND images, letting you:

- **Preserve character consistency** across multiple scenes (seriously, check out how it maintains identity through different environments!)
- **Make surgical local edits** without touching the rest of your image
- **Apply style references** from one image to generate entirely new scenes
- **Iterate at warp speed** with minimal latency

Three flavors of the model are available on our model playground:

- [**FLUX.1 Kontext \[pro\]**](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext?ref=blog.fal.ai) – Higher quality, medium speed, and medium cost
- [**FLUX.1 Kontext \[max\]**](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/flux-pro/kontext/max?ref=blog.fal.ai) – Maximum performance when you need the absolute best
- **FLUX.1 Kontext \[dev\]** (coming soon) – Fastest (in the world), and most cost affordable version of Kontext family; brought to you by the inference team at fal!
