### 3. Full app audit

Ran a comprehensive recon sweep across the entire app. Findings:

**Navigation map** — fully traced. Both houses reachable for ages 4–9. Age routing:
≤3 → placeholder, 4–6 → Giraffe house, 7–9 → Chicken house, 10–12 → LynseyHouseScreen,
13+ → placeholder.

**House status** — both houses more complete than previously documented:

- Giraffe House: 8 rooms, all reachable, all navigation wired

**Sensory trio gaps:**
- Music Corner exists but uses emoji tiles + text log, not the Humming Stones mechanic
- Calm Lagoon has video + turtle but no cursor/touch-following wave interaction

**Persistence gap**: Sleep and Energy use `SharedPreferences` (global, not per-child).
Mood, Worry, Check-in use Hive (per-child). Migration needed.

**Nutrition hub tile** routes to `CookingScreen`, not `NutritionScreen` — likely
intentional but worth confirming.

**LynseyHouseScreen (teen house)**: `FabInsightsScreen` (trend charts) only reachable
via this route — not accessible from main hub or parent dashboard.

### 4. Removed adult Nova Universe modules from teen house

`NovaRecoveryModuleScreen` and `NovaEvidenceNotesScreen` are adult health OS modules
(built for the separate Nova Universe project) that had been wired into `LynseyHouseScreen`
as the "Recovery Zone" and "Evidence Vault" tiles. Both screens are adult-facing
(pacing/flare language, benefits evidence framing), have no data persistence
(in-memory only), and are wrong audience for a child health app.

Removed:
- Recovery Zone tile → `NovaRecoveryModuleScreen`
- Evidence Vault tile → `NovaEvidenceNotesScreen`
- My Toolkit tile's leftover link → `NovaRecoveryModuleScreen` (tile kept, `onTap: () {}`)
- Both imports removed from `LynseyHouseScreen`
- `itemCount` 6 → 4

Screen files untouched on disk. Removals are reversible.
