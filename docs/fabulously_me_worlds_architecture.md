# Fabulously Me Worlds Architecture

## Purpose

Fabulously Me is the child and family wellbeing branch of the wider Nova universe.

It should stay cute, warm, magical, simple, character-led, and comforting.

Fabulously Me is not the container for all Nova modules. Wider Nova modules such as Recovery, Back Pain, Nutrition, Gastro, Adult Sleep/Fatigue, Clinician Export, and Insights belong to the broader Nova universe.

Fabulously Me focuses on children, parents/carers, feelings, routines, school support, sensory needs, calm plans, stories, and family wellbeing.

---

## Core Design Split

### Child-Facing Side

The child-facing side should feel:

- cute
- safe
- magical
- gentle
- visual
- character-led
- simple
- comforting
- not clinical

Child-facing language should use simple phrases like:

- How do you feel?
- What feels too much?
- What helps you feel safe?
- Pick a calm thing.
- Who can help?
- What happened today?

### Parent / Carer Side

The parent/carer side should feel:

- practical
- calm
- clear
- supportive
- organised
- not patronising
- useful when the parent is stressed
- focused on patterns and support

Parent-facing sections should help answer:

- What is happening?
- What triggers it?
- What helps?
- What makes it worse?
- What should school know?
- What should be mentioned at an appointment?
- What pattern is showing over time?

---

## Fabulously Me World Structure

Fabulously Me should be organised as several connected child/family wellbeing worlds.

Fabulously Me
- Main Calm World
- Sleep World
- Sensory World
- School World
- Big Feelings World
  - Worry House
- Parent / Carer Corner

Each world can contain:

1. A cute visual scene
2. A simple child check-in
3. A calm story, activity, or character moment
4. A parent/carer note section
5. Later: summary/export support

---

# 1. Main Calm World

## Purpose

The Main Calm World is the home base for Fabulously Me.

It should feel like the safe place the child returns to.

## Child Features

- Daily feeling check-in
- Character greeting
- Calm choice
- “What do I need?” button
- Simple mood options
- Gentle visual world
- Safe people reminder
- Positive routine message

## Parent / Carer Features

- Today’s feeling summary
- Notes about what helped
- Notes about what was hard
- Pattern over the week
- Quick parent reflection

## Example Child Prompts

- How are you feeling?
- What does your body need?
- Pick something calm.
- Who can help today?
- Do you want a story?

- What helped
- What made it worse

- Pattern summary later

## Example Child Prompts

- Do you want space or comfort?
- What helps your body feel safe?

# 5. Big Feelings World

## Purpose

Big Feelings World supports anger, sadness, anxiety, frustration, shutdowns, meltdowns, and emotional recovery.

It should never shame the child. It should help the family understand what happened and what helps.

## Child Features

- Big feeling picker
- Calm plan
- Character-guided breathing
- “I need space” option
- “I need help” option
- Safe person reminder
- Story after big feelings
- Recovery choice

## Parent / Carer Features

- Meltdown/shutdown notes
- What happened before
- Trigger notes
- What helped
- What did not help
- Recovery time
- Pattern tracking
- School/appointment notes later

## Example Child Prompts

- Is the feeling big?
- Where is the feeling in your body?
- Do you need space?
- Do you need help?
- Do you want quiet?
- What calm thing should we try?

## Parent Notes

- What happened before
- Trigger
- Early signs
- What helped
- What made it worse
- Recovery time
- What to try next time

## Worry House

Worry House belongs inside Big Feelings World, but it can also link to Sleep World and School World.

It is a safe place for children to put worries so they do not have to carry them alone.

### Child Features

- Put a worry in the house
- Choose the worry type:
  - school worry
  - bedtime worry
  - friend worry
  - body worry
  - loud/busy worry
  - family worry
  - “I don’t know” worry
- Choose what to do next:
  - talk to grown-up
  - park it for later
  - calm first
  - have a story
  - ask for help
- Character reassurance
- Bedtime worry box option

### Parent / Carer Features

- Repeated worry tracker
- Worry type notes
- Time of day
- What helped
- What made it worse
- School/SENCO relevance
- Appointment/support note later

### Example Child Prompts

- What worry shall we put in the Worry House?
- Is this a school worry?
- Is this a bedtime worry?
- Do you want to talk, park it, or calm first?
- Who can help with this worry?
- Shall Miss Chicken Lips keep it safe for now?

### Parent Notes

- What worry came up?
- Has this worry happened before?
- What was happening before it?
- Did the child need reassurance, space, routine, or support?
- Does this need a school note?

# 6. Parent / Carer Corner

## Purpose

Parent / Carer Corner is the practical support area for grown-ups.

It should help parents organise what they notice, what helps, what school needs to know, and what to raise in appointments.

This is not a clinical diagnosis tool. It is a support, tracking, and preparation space.

## Parent / Carer Features

- Pattern tracker
- Sensory profile
- Routine notes
- Meltdown/shutdown support notes
- School/SENCO notes
- Appointment prep
- What helps list
- What makes things harder list
- Parent stress/support notes
- Summary/export later

## Key Parent Questions

- What is happening?
- When does it happen?
- What happens before it?
- What helps?
- What makes it worse?
- How long does recovery take?
- What should school know?
- What should be asked at an appointment?
- What support does the child need?

## School / SENCO Prep

Parent / Carer Corner should help prepare:

- child needs summary
- sensory needs
- classroom support notes
- transition support notes
- social/friendship notes
- lunch/playground/toilet concerns
- what helps the child regulate
- questions for teacher/SENCO
- evidence of patterns over time

---

## Autism / Neurodivergent Support Direction

Fabulously Me should support autism and neurodivergent family needs gently.

It should not diagnose.

It should help families track:

- sensory triggers
- overwhelm signs
- meltdown/shutdown patterns
- routines
- transitions
- school difficulties
- sleep difficulties
- communication preferences
- what helps the child feel safe

The app should help parents feel less lost by giving them a simple place to keep information together.

---

## Safety Boundaries

Fabulously Me should not:

- diagnose autism, ADHD, anxiety, or any condition
- replace GP, SENCO, school, CAMHS, paediatrician, therapist, or emergency support
- give crisis-level advice beyond safe signposting
- shame children for behaviour
- treat meltdowns as naughty behaviour
- force one-size-fits-all solutions

Fabulously Me can:

- help children express feelings
- help families track patterns
- help parents prepare notes
- support calm routines
- help explain what helps
- organise school/appointment information
- encourage seeking proper support when needed
