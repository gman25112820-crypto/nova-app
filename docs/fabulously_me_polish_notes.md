# Fabulously Me — Visual Polish Notes

Parking lot for known later polish items. None of these are blockers.
Work through when there is a dedicated polish slot.

---

## Scene layout / characters

- Reduce oversized foreground character scale slightly — front-row characters
  can feel too large relative to the background houses and trees.
- Spread right giraffe family spacing — Theo, Ollie, and Dad Giraffe sit
  close together; giving them a little more horizontal breathing room
  would improve readability.
- Check and remove any tiny black accidental marks on roofs or walls —
  some builds show small dark artefacts that are likely unintentional
  paint calls or rounding errors.

## Right house

- Soften or redesign the right house open side panel / door area later —
  the exposed left face of the right house can look incomplete at certain
  viewport sizes.
- Review right house 3D side face colour against the overall scene palette.

## Depth and grounding

- Improve depth layering so characters feel properly grounded on the path —
  consider a subtle per-character ground shadow that scales with depth.
- Review z-order of mid-foreground characters against the gate structure.

## Scene direction

Keep Fabulously Me:
- cute, warm, gentle, and magical
- simple and uncluttered
- character-led and child-friendly
- comforting and safe-feeling

Do not drift toward:
- clinical / medical look
- dark or anxious tone
- complex UI that overwhelms a child
