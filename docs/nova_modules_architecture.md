\# Nova Modules Architecture



\## Purpose



Nova is the wider personal wellbeing, recovery, health-pattern, and evidence-support platform.



Fabulously Me is one child/family branch inside Nova.



This document covers the wider Nova modules outside Fabulously Me.



Nova should remain individual-owned, privacy-first, consent-led, and practical.



Nova should help people track patterns, understand what affects them, prepare for appointments, and export useful summaries when they choose.



Nova should not diagnose, prescribe, replace clinicians, or share data without consent.



\---



\## Core Rule



Every Nova module should answer:



1\. What is happening?

2\. How often does it happen?

3\. How bad is it?

4\. What triggers it?

5\. What helps?

6\. What makes it worse?

7\. How does it affect daily life?

8\. What should I safely do next?

9\. What evidence should I save?

10\. What should I ask at an appointment?



\---



\# 1. Nova Recovery



\## Purpose



Nova Recovery helps users track recovery, pacing, energy, flare-ups, routines, and rebuilding after difficult periods.



This can support physical recovery, mental recovery, addiction recovery, post-flare recovery, and general life rebuilding.



\## Core Sections



\- Daily recovery check-in

\- Energy score

\- Fatigue score

\- Pain/flare score

\- Mood impact

\- Routine tracker

\- What helped today

\- What made today harder

\- Safe next step

\- Weekly recovery pattern

\- Export summary later



\## Fields



\- Date

\- Recovery state

\- Energy 0-10

\- Fatigue 0-10

\- Pain 0-10

\- Mood

\- Sleep quality

\- Main trigger

\- What helped

\- What made it worse

\- Safe next action

\- Notes



\## Safety Boundary



Recovery progress does not mean doing more every day.



Recovery can mean pacing better, stopping earlier, avoiding triggers, resting properly, asking for help, or recovering faster after a hard day.

\# 7. Nova Insights



\## Purpose



Nova Insights finds useful patterns across modules.



It should help answer:



\- What changed?

\- What helped?

\- What made things worse?

\- What keeps repeating?

\- What should I ask at my next appointment?

\- What evidence is missing?



\## Core Sections



\- Weekly pattern

\- Monthly pattern

\- Triggers

\- Helpful actions

\- Worsening actions

\- Evidence gaps

\- Questions to ask

\- Export preparation



\## Safety Boundary



Insights should be phrased as observations, not diagnosis.



Example:



“Pain appears worse on poor sleep days.”



Not:



“You have a sleep-related pain disorder.”



\---



\# 8. Nova Clinician Export



\## Purpose



Nova Clinician Export turns logs into clean summaries for appointments, support services, school, benefits, or personal records.



The user chooses what to export.



\## Export Types



\- GP summary

\- Physio summary

\- Pain clinic summary

\- Dietitian summary

\- Mental health support summary

\- PIP / benefit evidence summary

\- School/SENCO summary

\- Carer support summary



\## Export Structure



\- Date range

\- Main issues

\- Frequency

\- Severity

\- Triggers

\- What helped

\- What made it worse

\- Daily life impact

\- Questions for appointment

\- Notes user wants to share



\## Safety Boundary



Exports are user-owned notes.



Nova should not automatically send anything.



\---



\## Shared Design Pattern



Every module should use the same friendly structure:



1\. Header

2\. “What this helps with”

3\. Quick check-in card

4\. Recent notes/history card

5\. What helped / what made it worse

6\. Safe next step

7\. Export/summary preview later



\---



\## Visual Style



The wider Nova modules should be:



\- grown-up

\- calm

\- practical

\- clean

\- warm

\- not childish

\- not clinical-heavy

\- not scary



They can still feel like Nova, but they should not use the full cute Fabulously Me style.



\---



\## Build Order



Recommended order:



1\. Recovery

2\. Back Pain

3\. Nutrition

4\. Gastro

5\. Cooking

6\. Sleep/Fatigue

8\. Clinician Export



Build these as separate screens first.
